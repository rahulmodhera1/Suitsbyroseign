# Brand assets

- `logo-white.png` — the supplied lockup, used as-is throughout the site.
- `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-16.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` — generated from a tight crop of the crown mark within `logo-white.png` (the full wordmark isn't legible at browser-tab size). No redrawing — the crown was cropped directly from the supplied artwork.
- `og-image.jpg` — 1200×630 social share card, the logo centred on the ink ground.

To replace the logo, upload a new `logo-white.png` here and re-run `python3 scripts/generate-favicons.py` (if present) or ask for updated icons.
